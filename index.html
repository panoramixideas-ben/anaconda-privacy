ANACONDA – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ANACONDA, a mobile puzzle game, handles information when you use the application. We are committed to protecting users' privacy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, the following information may be processed:

  • Anonymous account information: Firebase Authentication may create an anonymous user identifier to enable access to online features.
  • Game information: Your chosen username or nickname, leaderboard scores, game results and completion times may be stored using Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Technical information: Firebase and other service providers may process technical information such as device information, app version, diagnostic data and service usage information.

ANACONDA does not require you to provide your real name, postal address or telephone number to use its anonymous account feature.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Create and manage anonymous game accounts.
  • Display and maintain online leaderboards.
  • Save game results and progress where applicable.
  • Maintain, secure and improve the application.
  • Diagnose technical problems.

4. Third-Party Services

ANACONDA may use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore: These Google services are used for anonymous authentication and online leaderboard data.
    Firebase Privacy Information
  • Google AdMob: If advertisements are enabled, AdMob may process information such as device identifiers, IP address and advertising interaction data to provide, measure and protect advertisements.
    Google Privacy Policy

These third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

5. Advertising

ANACONDA may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on the user's location, applicable laws and consent choices, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party service providers described in this policy when necessary to operate the application, provide online features, display advertisements or maintain security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's features and maintain leaderboard records.

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your game account, contact us using the email address below. We will review and process your request in accordance with applicable requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

We take children's privacy seriously. ANACONDA does not knowingly request children's personal contact information. The handling of advertising and other data is subject to applicable child protection and privacy requirements.

9. Security

We rely on appropriate technical and organizational measures and the security features provided by our service providers to help protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its services change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
